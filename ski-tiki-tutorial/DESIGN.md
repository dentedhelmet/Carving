# Ski Tiki — Lodge Workshop

## Style Prompt
A playful ski-lodge field guide built around real vertical workshop footage. Cream paper panels, deep brown type, burnt-orange chapter numbers and understated pine-green details frame the wood grain. Large slab-serif titles feel like a hand-painted lodge sign; crisp monospaced notes feel like instructions pencilled on a workbench. Let the carving and the maker carry the personality. Portrait 1080 × 1920, approximately 80 seconds, clear narration and gentle editorial transitions.

## Colors
- Paper cream `#F4EBDD`: title panels and caption backgrounds.
- Timber brown `#38271E`: primary lettering and dark dividers.
- Burnt orange `#A84623`: chapter numbers, emphasis and progress.
- Pine green `#345447`: secondary labels and lodge details.
- Warm sand `#D9C3A4`: rules and restrained decorative marks.

## Typography
- **Bree Serif**, 400: large, friendly lodge-sign headlines, at least 70px.
- **IBM Plex Mono**, 400/600: practical instructions, subtitles and chapter notes, at least 28px; captions 38–42px.

## Motion
Short 0.4-second crossfades connect the process. All chapter lettering enters gently; a few title accents have a small playful overshoot. The original handheld footage stays full and legible. Photos receive a restrained slow push. No fast flashing or effects that obscure tool work.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon ski-resort graphics, stock snow footage or invented carving stages.
- No heavy color filters or textures over the original footage.
- No subtitles across the active tool or carving area.
- No dense paragraphs, spinning titles or transitions that overpower the work.
- No implied proof of a finish product, wood species or unseen technique.
